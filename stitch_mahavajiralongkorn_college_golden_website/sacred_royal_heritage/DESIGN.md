## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal dignity, monastic serenity, and intellectual rigor of Mahavajiralongkorn Pali Theravada College (มหาวชิราลงกรณบาลีเถรวาทราชวิทยาลัย). It bridges classical Thai royal court aesthetics with disciplined Buddhist scholasticism and contemporary digital clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes:
- **Sacred Reverence (คารวะปสาทะ):** Dignified calm, reverence for sacred Pali canon studies, and royal patronage.
- **Intellectual Nobility:** Crisp, scholarly layout, deliberate hierarchy, and unhurried visual cadence.
- **Serenity & Warmth:** Soft ivory surfaces infused with monastic saffron warmth, avoiding sterile coldness.

### Visual Style
A tailored fusion of **Royal Classical Minimalism** and **Tactile Regalia**:
- Ultra-refined editorial whitespace anchored by high-contrast Royal Midnight structural elements.
- Fine-gauge metallic gold framing (Kanok-inspired linework and hairline golden borders) evoking traditional Thai manuscript gilding (*lai rod nam*).
- Architectural restraint: decorative flourishes are strictly secondary to clear theological and curricular readability.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from royal insignia, canonical Tipitaka manuscript bindings, and Theravada monastic robes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Royal Gold — #C5A059):** Applied to badges, active nav indicators, high-status borders, illuminated headings, and primary calls to action. Accompanied by `#D4AF37` for radiant metallic highlights and `#996515` for deep engraved press states.
- **Secondary (Buddhist Saffron / Civara Amber — #7A3803 & #8B4513):** Rooted in traditional dyed monk robes. Anchors secondary interactive controls, academic section tags, liturgical notices, and theological highlights.
- **Tertiary (Royal Midnight Navy — #0B192C):** Represents royal patronage, deep night contemplation, and uncompromising contrast. Serves as deep backgrounds for ceremonial banners, footers, structural navigation bars, and primary text.
- **Neutral (Warm Ivory & Pure Cream — #FAF8F5 / #FFFDF9):** The canvas across light surfaces. Mimics archival mulberry paper (*samut khoi*), preventing digital glare while offering a soft, reverent backdrop.

### Application Rules
- **Light Theme Default:** Academic portals and reading screens default to Warm Ivory backgrounds (`#FAF8F5`) with card surfaces on Pure Cream (`#FFFDF9`) and high-contrast typography in Deep Slate/Midnight (`#0B192C`).
- **Ceremonial Inversions:** Royal proclamations, ordination notices, and monastic registries may utilize full Royal Midnight surfaces (`#0B192C`) edged with hairline `#C5A059` metallic strokes.

## Typography

The typographical pairing provides institutional gravity while honoring bilingual Pali-Thai-English pedagogical standards.

### Type Roles
- **Headlines & Titles (Noto Serif):** Delivers classical editorial dignity, aligning naturally with serifed Thai scholastic traditions (comparable to Noto Serif Thai and Sarabun). It commands respect in degree conferred announcements, canonical Pali citations, and institutional headers.
- **Body & Labels (Be Vietnam Pro):** Chosen for its tall x-height, open apertures, and exceptional readability on mobile devices. It renders Pali Romanization (with macrons and underdots) with pristine clarity and balances cleanly alongside contemporary loopless/looped Thai body fonts (e.g., Bai Jamjuree / Sarabun).

### Formatting Rules
- Never pair Noto Serif in italic weights with Pali transliteration terms; use subtle gold tinting or medium weight for emphasis.
- Heading elements must maintain a minimum 1.3 line-height factor to accommodate Thai tone marks (*วรรณยุกต์*) and Pali diacritics without vertical clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop (max-width `1280px`) that simplifies to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens. 

### Spacing Principles
- **Monastic Balance (สัปปายะ):** Generous breathing room around scholarly passages and ceremonial imagery. Vertical padding prioritizes `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` between major pedagogical modules.
- **Mobile-First Compactness:** Compact structural spacing on phone screens ensures Buddhist monastic daily schedules (*Patimokkha* recitations, lectures, meal hours) remain glanceable without excessive scrolling.
- **Alignment:** Center-aligned ceremonial banners transition strictly into left-aligned scholarly study texts for uninterrupted sustained reading.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead creating depth through **Tonal Layering** and **Lustrous Metallic Framing**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface:** Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
2. **Elevated Surface (Cards, Reading Panels):** Crisp Cream (`#FFFDF9`) bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.28)`.
3. **Floating Overlays (Modals, Context Menus, Royal Bulletins):** Layered above with an ambient golden shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`.
4. **Ceremonial Prominence:** Midnight containers (`#0B192C`) utilize inner-edge radiance: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry. 

Elements employ slight rounding (`0.25rem` / 4px on badges and buttons, `0.5rem` / 8px on content cards, and `0.75rem` / 12px on institutional modals). This reflects the crisp edges of hand-bound canonical volumes and stone stelae (*sila charoek*), rejecting overly bubbly silhouettes in favor of formal poise and structural integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Royal Golden Standard):** Filled with Royal Gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C5A059 100%)`), text in Deep Midnight (`#0B192C`), font weight 600. Finished with a subtle `1px solid #996515` bottom bezel for tactile presence.
- **Secondary (Civara Saffron):** Filled with `#7A3803`, text in `#FFFDF9`, hovering to `#5C2B02`. Used for academic downloads, enrollment tasks, and liturgical notices.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A059` border, text in `#7A3803`. On midnight surfaces, text and border adopt `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Reading Panels
- Structured on `#FFFDF9` with a delicate `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` perimeter.
- Top card headers may include an optional 2px accent strip in Royal Gold or Buddhist Saffron to signify faculty division (e.g., Tipitaka Studies, Pali Linguistics, Vinaya Research).

### Badges & Chips
- **Monastic & Academic Chips:** Height of 26px, padding `0 10px`, roundedness `0.25rem`. Background `#FAF8F5` bordered with `1px solid #C5A059`. Text set in label-sm in `#7A3803`.
- **Royal Patronage Emblems:** Dark navy pill with metallic gold text and an embedded miniature gold lotus glyph.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D6CEBE`. 
- Focused state transitions border to `#C5A059` with a warm halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.20)`).
- Labels are styled in `Be Vietnam Pro` 13px bold `#0B192C`.

### Lotus Iconography & Thai Motif Dividers
- Section separators incorporate subtle golden hairline bars interrupted at center by a stylized golden 8-petaled lotus (*Padma* / ดอกบัวโสฬส) or Dhamma wheel motif (`#C5A059`).
- Icon sets must maintain a consistent 1.5px stroke weight rendered in metallic gold or midnight ink.
